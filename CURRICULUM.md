# Complete 26-Week Piano Curriculum

Start: Monday, 14 September 2026. End: Saturday, 13 March 2027.

The curriculum assumes beginner level. Each week includes technique, theory, ear work and an actual musical reward. Choruses begin immediately.

---

## Phase 1. Immediate foundations and first choruses

### Week 1, 14-19 Sep: Know the keyboard and make music immediately
**Primary video:** Pianote first lesson https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4__cIAvrWHw

**Monday:** 5 min posture and relaxed hand shape. 10 min identify groups of 2/3 black keys and locate every C. 10 min musical alphabet. 15 min five-finger C-D-E-F-G right hand, then left. 15 min begin the melody of *This Is the Day*. 5 min higher/lower ear game.

**Tuesday:** 5 min warm-up. 10 min name random white keys. 10 min five-finger pattern at 50-60 bpm. 10 min build C major from C-E-G. 20 min chorus practice, melody first, then C chord on strong beats where comfortable. 5 min record 30 seconds.

**Wednesday:** Watch the relevant section of https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEtukfFv3Wk. Learn finger numbers and basic quarter-note pulse. Clap 4/4 for 5 min. Play C-F-G single bass notes with left hand and simple right-hand melody for 20 min.

**Thursday:** Learn F and G major triads by note name. Drill C-F-G-C 20 times slowly. Ear: listen to your own C-F-G and identify which sounds most settled as "home". Reading: locate middle C on basic notation using https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PyOKefHURsg for no more than 10 min.

**Friday:** Play *This Is the Day* from beginning to end at a slow steady pulse. Do not stop for mistakes. Repeat three times. Final 10 min: play C-F-G-C as whole-note chords.

**Saturday:** No new lesson. 10 min keyboard naming, 10 min five-finger patterns, 15 min C-F-G changes, 20 min chorus, 5 min recording. **Pass:** find C/F/G quickly and play a recognisable complete chorus.

### Week 2, 21-26 Sep: C major scale, pulse and first accompaniment
**Primary video:** Pianote beginner lesson https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tEtukfFv3Wk

**Monday:** Learn C major one-octave right-hand fingering slowly. 10 min separate thumb-under movement. 15 min scale, 10 min C/F/G triads, 20 min *What a Mighty God We Serve* melody or chord skeleton.

**Tuesday:** C major left hand one octave. Metronome 50 bpm, one note per click. Chord drill C-G-F-C. Chorus: left-hand roots, right-hand simple triads.

**Wednesday:** Rhythm: quarter notes, half notes, whole notes and rests. Clap before playing. Play C chord as whole notes, then half notes. Add simple two-chord/three-chord accompaniment to the chorus.

**Thursday:** Ear: tonic versus non-tonic. Play C then random white note, return to C. Reading: treble landmark notes around middle C for 10 min. Technique: both hands separately C scale.

**Friday:** Perform Week 1 and Week 2 choruses back-to-back. Aim for no stops. Record and listen once without touching the piano; write the single biggest timing problem.

**Saturday:** 15 min scale, 15 min chord changes, 25 min both choruses, 5 min recording. **Pass:** C scale hands separately and C-F-G progression at 60 bpm in whole notes.

### Week 3, 28 Sep-3 Oct: Intervals, melody confidence and hand independence
**Primary reading supplement:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PyOKefHURsg

**Monday:** Learn seconds, thirds, fourths and fifths on the keyboard. Play C-D, C-E, C-F, C-G. Sing or hum before playing. 20 min *He's Got the Whole World in His Hands* melody.

**Tuesday:** Five-finger contrary-motion drill in C. Left hand holds C whole note while right hand plays five-note patterns. Then switch to F and G bass notes under melody fragments.

**Wednesday:** Build major triads using root-3rd-5th. Construct C, F, G without diagrams. Then build D major and compare its F-sharp. Do not yet memorise all keys.

**Thursday:** Rhythm: 4/4 counting aloud, 1-2-3-4. Play left-hand roots on beats 1 and 3, right-hand chords on 1. Ear: recognise step versus skip.

**Friday:** Apply to chorus. First play melody only, then chords only, then combine left-hand root plus right-hand melody where possible. Do not force full two-hand complexity if pulse collapses.

**Saturday:** Review Weeks 1-3 repertoire. **Pass:** identify intervals 2nd/3rd/4th/5th visually on keyboard and play three choruses recognisably.

### Week 4, 5-10 Oct: Major and minor, first monthly performance
**Supplement:** Bill Hilton chords https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C1i-cFx7__M, only major/minor section.

**Monday:** Learn major versus minor triad formula by semitone structure or major/minor third relationship. Convert C to Cm, F to Fm, G to Gm.

**Tuesday:** Drill C, Am, F, G, the classic 1-6-4-5 family. 60 bpm, one chord every four beats. Learn bass roots separately.

**Wednesday:** Start *Give Me Oil in My Lamp*. Find likely home note/key, then play basic triads. Keep arrangement simple.

**Thursday:** Ear: major versus minor quality. Play pairs yourself and label before looking. Reading: 10 min landmark-note review. Technique: C scale both hands separately at 60 bpm.

**Friday:** Month-1 mini-performance: four choruses, 30-60 seconds each. No tutorial open.

**Saturday:** Record the Month-1 test from `PROGRESS.md`. **Pass:** basic keyboard geography, C scale, C/F/G and Am, steady 4/4, at least two choruses from memory.

## Phase 2. Keys, chords, inversions and smoother playing

### Week 5, 12-17 Oct: Key of G and the number system begins
**Monday:** G major scale, identify F-sharp. Learn 1-2-3-4-5-6-7 scale degrees. Build G, C, D, Em.

**Tuesday:** Play 1-4-5-1 in G. Then 1-5-6-4 = G-D-Em-C. Say the numbers aloud while playing.

**Wednesday:** Begin *I Will Enter His Gates*. First basic chord skeleton in G or another comfortable key.

**Thursday:** Ear: sing scale degrees 1-2-3-2-1 and 1-3-5. Rhythm: change chords every 4 beats, then every 2 beats.

**Friday:** Chorus application. Alternate left-hand root and fifth where comfortable.

**Saturday:** Review C and G. **Pass:** G scale hands separately and 1-5-6-4 in G at 60 bpm.

### Week 6, 19-24 Oct: Key of F, sustain pedal and lyrical playing
**Monday:** F major scale, identify B-flat. Build F, Bb, C, Dm.

**Tuesday:** Pedal basics: depress after chord attack, release/re-pedal on harmony change. Practise C-F-G-C slowly without blur.

**Wednesday:** Start *Amazing Grace*. Melody first, then harmony. Keep pedal light.

**Thursday:** Transpose 1-4-5 from C to F by numbers. Ear: sing tonic before touching keyboard.

**Friday:** Play *Amazing Grace* once melody only, once chords only, once combined as simply as necessary.

**Saturday:** Review C/G/F scales and three key families. **Pass:** clean pedal changes on a slow four-chord sequence.

### Week 7, 26-31 Oct: Inversions and voice leading
**Supplement:** MangoldProject beginner course https://www.classcentral.com/course/youtube-piano-lessons-for-beginners-102467

**Monday:** Learn root position, first inversion and second inversion using C major.

**Tuesday:** Invert F and G. Find the nearest C-F-G voicings without jumping across the keyboard.

**Wednesday:** Begin *Blessed Assurance*. Use simple inversions on repeated chord changes.

**Thursday:** Voice-leading drill: C -> F/C-style close position -> G/B-style close movement where musically sensible. Focus on minimal hand travel, not labels alone.

**Friday:** Repertoire day. Play Week 7 chorus with at least two inversions.

**Saturday:** Record root-position version then inversion version. **Pass:** all three inversions of C/F/G from memory.

### Week 8, 2-7 Nov: Key of D and rhythmic accompaniment
**Monday:** D major scale, F-sharp and C-sharp. Build D, G, A, Bm.

**Tuesday:** 1-5-6-4 in D. Right hand uses closest inversions. Left hand roots.

**Wednesday:** Begin *Standing on the Promises*. Experiment with block chords versus repeated rhythmic chords.

**Thursday:** Rhythm drill: whole-note chords, half-note chords, then 1-and-2-and counting. Do not speed beyond clean coordination.

**Friday:** Four-key review C/G/F/D. Play one progression in each without written chord names, using numbers.

**Saturday:** Month-2 test from `PROGRESS.md`.

## Phase 3. Number system, transposition, ear and accompaniment

### Week 9, 9-14 Nov: 1-5-6-4 and 6-4-1-5 fluency
**Monday:** In C and G, play both progressions slowly with inversions.

**Tuesday:** Add left-hand root-fifth pattern. Metronome 60 bpm.

**Wednesday:** Start *As the Deer*. Identify which familiar number movements appear.

**Thursday:** Ear: play 1 then 5, 1 then 4, 1 then 6; learn their directional feel. Hum bass movement before playing.

**Friday:** Chorus with consistent accompaniment texture.

**Saturday:** **Pass:** both progressions in C/G/F without stopping.

### Week 10, 16-21 Nov: Chord spelling and *Give Thanks*
**Monday:** Spell diatonic triads in C: C Dm Em F G Am Bdim. Understand why chord quality changes by scale degree.

**Tuesday:** Repeat diatonic chords in G. Do not memorise isolated pictures; relate each chord to the scale.

**Wednesday:** Begin *Give Thanks*. Write the progression in numbers after learning the basic chords.

**Thursday:** Ear: play random diatonic C chords and identify major/minor quality. Reading: 10 min treble/bass landmarks.

**Friday:** Play *Give Thanks* while saying numbers quietly before each chord change.

**Saturday:** Review and recording.

### Week 11, 23-28 Nov: Key of A and expressive accompaniment
**Monday:** A major scale. Build A, D, E, F#m.

**Tuesday:** 1-4-5 and 1-5-6-4 in A slowly. Do not prioritise speed.

**Wednesday:** Start *I Love You Lord*. Work on dynamics: softer verse-like texture, stronger peak.

**Thursday:** Pedal and phrasing. Change pedal with harmony, not mechanically every beat.

**Friday:** Record two versions: plain block chords and a more flowing accompaniment.

**Saturday:** **Pass:** clear dynamic contrast without tempo collapse.

### Week 12, 30 Nov-5 Dec: Key of Bb, left-hand patterns and *Majesty*
**Monday:** Bb major scale. Build Bb, Eb, F, Gm.

**Tuesday:** Left-hand patterns: root only, root-fifth, octave where comfortable. Avoid tension.

**Wednesday:** Start *Majesty*. Use right-hand inversions and left-hand roots/fifths.

**Thursday:** Transpose 1-4-5 from F to Bb. Ear: hear 5 -> 1 resolution.

**Friday:** Play *Majesty* and one earlier chorus in Bb or F.

**Saturday:** Review all six priority keys so far: C, G, D, A, F, Bb.

### Week 13, 7-12 Dec: Consolidation and mini-set
**Monday:** Scales C/G/D/A/F/Bb, one clean octave each hand separately.

**Tuesday:** Progression circuit: 1-4-5, 1-5-6-4, 6-4-1-5 in C/G/F/D.

**Wednesday:** Repertoire repair. Pick the two weakest pieces from Weeks 1-12 and simplify them until they flow.

**Thursday:** Ear/transposition test: find tonic for one familiar chorus and shift it from C to G or F.

**Friday:** Four-chorus mini-set, no tutorial.

**Saturday:** Month-3 test from `PROGRESS.md`.

## Phase 4. Gospel vocabulary, sevenths and stronger harmony

### Week 14, 14-19 Dec: Seventh chords
**Supplement:** Bill Hilton https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C1i-cFx7__M

**Monday:** Build C7, Cmaj7, Cm7. Understand the different sevenths.

**Tuesday:** Build G7, Fmaj7, Dm7, Am7. Use them inside familiar progressions.

**Wednesday:** Begin *Oh How I Love Jesus*. Keep basic harmony first, then replace selected triads with seventh colours.

**Thursday:** Ear: compare major triad versus major 7 versus dominant 7. Do not demand perfect identification yet.

**Friday:** Play chorus with one intentional seventh chord per phrase where appropriate.

**Saturday:** **Pass:** build major 7, minor 7 and dominant 7 from C/F/G without a chart.

### Week 15, 21-26 Dec: Walk-ups, walk-downs and gospel feel
**Supplement:** Pianote gospel lesson https://www.pianote.com/blog/?p=7490

**Monday:** Learn scale-wise bass walk 1-2-3-4 and 4-3-2-1 in C.

**Tuesday:** Add simple harmonisation above a walk only after bass is stable.

**Wednesday:** Begin *I Have Decided to Follow Jesus*. Add one simple walk between stable destination chords.

**Thursday:** Rhythm: anticipate a chord slightly before beat 1 only after steady basic version is secure.

**Friday:** Compare plain version versus lightly gospel-coloured version.

**Saturday:** **Pass:** one tasteful walk-up and one walk-down without losing form.

### Week 16, 28 Dec-2 Jan: 2-5-1 introduction
**Monday:** In C, Dm7-G7-Cmaj7. Learn functions: 2 leads to 5, 5 resolves to 1.

**Tuesday:** 2-5-1 in F: Gm7-C7-Fmaj7. Then G: Am7-D7-Gmaj7.

**Wednesday:** Begin *To God Be the Glory*. Identify strong cadential moments.

**Thursday:** Ear: listen for tension on 5 and release on 1. Play repeated 5-1 pairs.

**Friday:** Add a simple 2-5 approach to one cadence only where it fits.

**Saturday:** **Pass:** 2-5-1 in C/F/G slowly from memory.

### Week 17, 4-9 Jan: Richer voicing without clutter
**Monday:** Review inversions of seventh chords. Keep melody/top note clear.

**Tuesday:** Learn sus2/sus4 and add9 concept from Bill Hilton as colour tones, not replacements for harmonic understanding.

**Wednesday:** Begin *Great Is Thy Faithfulness*. First plain triads, then selective major7/add9/sus colour.

**Thursday:** Left hand: root or root-fifth only while right hand carries richer voicing. Avoid muddy low chords.

**Friday:** Record plain and enriched versions. Decide which changes genuinely sound better.

**Saturday:** Month-4 test from `PROGRESS.md`.

## Phase 5. Ear, melody plus harmony, transposition and applied gospel

### Week 18, 11-16 Jan: Passing chords with restraint
**Supplement:** Piano With Jonny https://pianowithjonny.com/piano-lessons/play-gospel-piano-the-6-step-beginner-guide/

**Monday:** Revisit 1-6-4-5 or 1-5-6-4 in C. Mark destination chords clearly.

**Tuesday:** Learn one secondary-dominant-style approach by sound and function. Do not stack several passing ideas.

**Wednesday:** Return to *As the Deer*. Add one passing movement between two known destinations.

**Thursday:** Ear: sing bass destination notes before adding passing motion.

**Friday:** Play basic version first, decorated version second.

**Saturday:** **Pass:** decorated version still keeps form, pulse and harmonic destination obvious.

### Week 19, 18-23 Jan: Melody plus chords
**Primary Chris Ebute lesson:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qp4VqcQ9Po4

**Monday:** Watch no more than 15-20 min. Practise right-hand melody with selected chord tones underneath.

**Tuesday:** Apply concept to the first phrase of *Amazing Grace*.

**Wednesday:** Extend to a second phrase. Left hand plays roots only.

**Thursday:** Separate melody-top-note awareness from inner harmony. Play top melody alone, then add one or two inner notes.

**Friday:** Full simple melody-plus-chord version.

**Saturday:** Record. **Pass:** melody remains clearly audible above chord support.

### Week 20, 25-30 Jan: Transposition as a real skill
**Monday:** Take a familiar 1-4-5 chorus in C and rewrite as numbers.

**Tuesday:** Play it in G. Use scale-degree logic rather than note-by-note shifting.

**Wednesday:** Play same chorus in F.

**Thursday:** Play it in D. Slow down if black-key geography causes errors.

**Friday:** Transpose one section of *Blessed Assurance* or another familiar chorus to a second key.

**Saturday:** **Pass:** one complete easy chorus in at least three keys.

### Week 21, 1-6 Feb: Learn a worship chorus partly by ear
**Monday:** Choose a simple familiar worship chorus. Before searching a tutorial, find the tonic by trial and singing.

**Tuesday:** Find melody starting notes and likely bass roots for the first phrase.

**Wednesday:** Guess number movement. Only now check a tutorial or chord chart to correct yourself.

**Thursday:** Relearn corrected progression without staring at the source.

**Friday:** Play the complete chorus from memory.

**Saturday:** Record and document what your ear got right/wrong.

### Week 22, 8-13 Feb: Gospel chorus by ear and 1-5-6-4 decoration
**Supplement:** Warren lesson https://pianolessonwithwarren.com/gospel-piano-passing-chords-for-the-1-5-6-4-progression/

**Monday:** Play plain 1-5-6-4 in G at 60-70 bpm.

**Tuesday:** Add inversions and smoother voice leading.

**Wednesday:** Add one approved passing movement from the supplementary lesson.

**Thursday:** Choose a simple gospel chorus and identify bass movement before checking the source.

**Friday:** Play simple chorus arrangement with no more than one or two decorations.

**Saturday:** Month-5 test from `PROGRESS.md`.

## Phase 6. Independence, all keys, arrangement and performance

### Week 23, 15-20 Feb: Extended chords and harmonic vocabulary
**Chris Ebute:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XmX0si6UWJs

**Monday:** Review major7, minor7, dominant7, add9, sus. Watch selected Chris Ebute sections.

**Tuesday:** Build extensions from chord tones in C and G. Name every note you add.

**Wednesday:** Apply one richer voicing to a familiar chorus.

**Thursday:** Ear: compare triad versus enriched voicing; identify whether colour helps or obscures melody.

**Friday:** Transposition project: one chorus in C/F/G/D.

**Saturday:** **Pass:** richer harmony does not require memorised hand shapes only; you can explain the chord's basic structure.

### Week 24, 22-27 Feb: Arrangement craft
**Monday:** Choose one familiar chorus. Design a 2-4 bar intro from its progression.

**Tuesday:** Create two textures: sparse verse-like and fuller chorus-like.

**Wednesday:** Design an ending using 5-1, 4-1 or a simple tag.

**Thursday:** Practise transitions among intro, main body and ending.

**Friday:** Perform full arrangement without stopping.

**Saturday:** Record full arrangement and critique timing, voicing, pedal and dynamics.

### Week 25, 1-6 Mar: Five-song set and pressure-proofing
**Monday:** Choose five strongest pieces. Play all five once, no stopping.

**Tuesday:** Repair only the two worst transitions or sections.

**Wednesday:** Metronome day. Practise difficult sections 10-20 bpm below performance tempo, then return to target tempo.

**Thursday:** Memory test: begin each piece from the middle, not only from the opening.

**Friday:** Full five-song set. No YouTube open.

**Saturday:** Record the set. Note recurring technical weakness rather than isolated mistakes.

### Week 26, 8-13 Mar: Final consolidation and performance
**Monday:** Major-scale survey across all 12 keys, hands separately. Priority keys C/G/D/A/F/Bb should be most fluent. Add Eb/E/Ab/B/Db/Gb as slower technical review if needed.

**Tuesday:** Chord survey: major/minor triads all keys; seventh, sus and add9 in common keys. Progression circuit in at least C/G/F/D/A/Bb.

**Wednesday:** Ear test: find tonic and likely first bass movement of two simple familiar songs before checking.

**Thursday:** Transposition test: one easy chorus in C/F/G/D. Melody-plus-chords test on one familiar piece.

**Friday:** Dress rehearsal: 10-15 minute final set, one take, no restart.

**Saturday, 13 Mar:** Final recording. Complete the Final Test in `PROGRESS.md`. Save the recording and write a short assessment under three headings: **What I can now do reliably**, **What remains weak**, **What the next six months should focus on**.

# Technique expectations by the end

The course does not promise virtuosity in six months. A realistic successful outcome is a practical lower-intermediate base with strong chord literacy and emerging ear skills. By the end, the learner should be able to play common major scales, common progressions in several keys, use inversions and seventh colours, accompany simple choruses, transpose easier material, and begin learning by ear.

# When to slow down

Do not push tempo if any of these appear:
- wrist or forearm tension;
- repeated wrong fingering;
- inability to count aloud;
- loss of pulse whenever chords change;
- dependence on visually copying the teacher's hands.

Reduce speed, isolate two beats or one chord change, repeat correctly five times, then rebuild the phrase.

# Minimum viable practice on a difficult day

If one hour is impossible, do 30 minutes rather than skip:
- 5 min scale
- 5 min chord drill
- 10 min week's hardest transition
- 10 min chorus from memory

Consistency across 26 weeks matters more than occasional marathon sessions.
