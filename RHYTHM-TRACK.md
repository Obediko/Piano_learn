# Rhythm and Groove Track

Harmony without time is not usable playing. This track runs in parallel with the main curriculum.

## How to practise rhythm

Always move through this order:
1. clap;
2. count aloud;
3. tap one note;
4. play one chord;
5. apply to progression;
6. apply to song.

If counting collapses when harmony is added, remove harmony and rebuild the rhythm first.

## Counting language

- Quarter notes: `1 2 3 4`
- Eighth notes: `1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &`
- Sixteenth notes later: `1 e & a 2 e & a 3 e & a 4 e & a`

## Weeks 1-4: steady pulse

### Week 1
- clap quarter notes at 50-60 bpm;
- count 1-2-3-4 aloud;
- play C chord on beat 1 only;
- pass: 60 seconds without losing metronome.

### Week 2
- distinguish quarter, half and whole-note durations;
- play chord on beats 1 and 3;
- pass: 16 bars without stopping.

### Week 3
- left-hand root on beats 1 and 3, right chord on beat 1;
- pass: maintain count while hands do different jobs.

### Week 4
- C-Am-F-G, one chord per bar;
- then one chord every 2 beats;
- pass: change without shortening the beat before a difficult chord.

## Weeks 5-8: subdivisions and accompaniment

### Week 5
- eighth-note counting `1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &`;
- clap first, then repeat one chord on quarters;
- pass: spoken subdivisions remain even.

### Week 6
- pedal changes on harmonic changes while pulse continues;
- pass: pedal does not cause hesitation.

### Week 7
- use nearest inversions to reduce timing disruption;
- pass: compare root-position vs inversion version; inversion version should be equally or more stable.

### Week 8
- repeated chord pattern: play on beats 1, 2, 3, 4;
- then try beat 1 plus the `&` of 2 if stable;
- pass: syncopated attempt never replaces the steady version.

## Weeks 9-13: groove foundation

### Week 9
- left root/fifth as half-note movement;
- right chord on beats 1 and 3;
- target 60-70 bpm.

### Week 10
- alternate whole-bar and half-bar harmonic rhythm;
- learn to feel when chord changes happen, not only what chord comes next.

### Week 11
- dynamics without tempo drift;
- play soft 8 bars, louder 8 bars, same bpm.

### Week 12
- left-hand octave/root-fifth pattern at steady half-note pulse;
- right hand uses sustained chords.

### Week 13
- progression circuit with no reset between keys;
- 1 bar pause allowed only when changing key, not within progression.

## Weeks 14-17: anticipation and gospel movement

### Week 14
- seventh chords in straight quarter-note pulse first;
- no rhythmic decoration until harmony is accurate.

### Week 15
- bass walk across four even beats;
- then try destination chord slightly before beat 1 only if original version is stable.

### Week 16
- 2-5-1: one chord per bar, then 2 beats on 2, 2 beats on 5, full bar on 1.

### Week 17
- sus/add9 colour without delaying chord changes;
- pass: added notes do not disturb pulse.

## Weeks 18-22: syncopation and accompaniment feel

### Week 18
- introduce one anticipatory chord on `& of 4` leading into next bar;
- do this only on a known progression.

### Week 19
- melody plus chords: melody rhythm stays primary, inner chord tones must not create extra unintended attacks.

### Week 20
- transpose while preserving identical rhythm;
- if rhythm changes because key is harder, slow all keys to the same lower tempo.

### Week 21
- copy rhythm by ear from a familiar chorus before copying pitches;
- clap the phrase first.

### Week 22
- gospel 1-5-6-4: plain version, then one rhythmic anticipation;
- pass: both versions remain recognisably the same groove.

## Weeks 23-26: performance rhythm

### Week 23
- switch between sparse and full textures without changing tempo.

### Week 24
- practise intro -> verse texture -> fuller section -> ending using one continuous metronome.

### Week 25
- full-set pressure test;
- recover after mistakes without stopping pulse.

### Week 26
- perform at musically appropriate tempo rather than fastest achievable tempo.

## Rhythm rescue protocol

When a passage falls apart:
1. remove pedal;
2. remove left hand;
3. reduce right hand to one chord or one note;
4. clap the rhythm;
5. count aloud;
6. play at 20 bpm slower;
7. rebuild one layer at a time.

## Pass standards

- Bronze: steady 30 seconds.
- Silver: steady 60 seconds with chord changes.
- Gold: steady 90 seconds with both hands and one intentional rhythmic variation.

Record the highest level reached each Saturday.
