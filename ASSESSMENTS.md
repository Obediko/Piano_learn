# Assessment System

This course uses practical tests, not vague impressions. The purpose is to decide what is secure, what needs repair, and whether the next layer is appropriate.

## Saturday recording rubric, 100 points

Score one complete take each Saturday.

### Pulse and continuity, 25
- 25: steady pulse, no restart, minor mistakes do not derail performance
- 20: one brief hesitation
- 15: several timing disruptions but piece remains intact
- 10: frequent stops
- 5: cannot maintain pulse

### Note/chord accuracy, 20
- 20: almost entirely correct
- 16: a few isolated errors
- 12: repeated errors in one section
- 8: errors throughout but harmony remains recognisable
- 4: harmony/melody often incorrect

### Chord transitions/coordination, 15
- 15: smooth changes
- 12: occasional late change
- 9: several awkward jumps
- 6: hand coordination repeatedly breaks
- 3: cannot complete two-hand version

### Technique and relaxation, 10
- 10: relaxed and controlled
- 8: mild tension under difficulty
- 6: recurring stiffness
- 4: visible strain affects playing
- 2: technique unsafe or highly unstable

### Rhythm/groove, 10
- 10: subdivision/groove is clear
- 8: mostly consistent
- 6: plain pulse stable but groove weak
- 4: inconsistent rhythmic pattern
- 2: rhythm frequently lost

### Musical control, 10
- 10: intentional dynamics, phrasing and pedal
- 8: some clear musical shaping
- 6: mostly mechanical but controlled
- 4: pedal/dynamics distract
- 2: no control beyond note survival

### Independence, 10
- 10: no tutorial/chord prompt needed
- 8: one brief check before starting
- 6: occasional prompts
- 4: frequent reference required
- 2: can only follow tutorial in real time

## Weekly interpretation

- 90-100: Excellent. Maintain and add one new layer.
- 80-89: Secure. Progress normally.
- 70-79: Functional but fragile. Progress while carrying one repair target.
- 60-69: Do not add complexity to the weak skill. Use `RECOVERY.md`.
- Below 60: simplify the arrangement and rebuild foundations before enrichment.

## Monthly practical exam format

Each monthly test contains five domains worth 20 points each:
1. technique;
2. harmony/theory;
3. rhythm;
4. ear/transposition;
5. repertoire/performance.

Pass threshold: 70/100 overall, with no domain below 10/20.

## Month 1 exam

### Technique, 20
- locate white notes quickly;
- C five-finger pattern;
- C major scale hands separately.

### Harmony, 20
- build C, F, G, Am without diagram;
- play C-F-G-C;
- distinguish major/minor construction.

### Rhythm, 20
- clap/count 4/4;
- play 16 bars at 60 bpm without losing pulse.

### Ear, 20
- higher/lower: 8/10 target;
- home/not-home recognition;
- major/minor: at least 7/10.

### Repertoire, 20
- two early choruses from memory;
- one uninterrupted recording.

## Month 2 exam

Technique: C/G/F/D scales, inversions of C/F/G.
Harmony: 1-4-5 and 1-5-6-4 in at least two keys.
Rhythm: chord changes at 60-70 bpm.
Ear: scale-degree direction and tonic resolution.
Repertoire: four choruses from memory; use pedal cleanly on one.

## Month 3 exam

Technique: C/G/D/A/F/Bb scales hands separately.
Harmony: 1-4-5, 1-5-6-4, 6-4-1-5 in C/G/F/D.
Rhythm: root-fifth + right-hand chord pattern.
Ear: recognise tonic and basic bass direction; transpose one easy chorus.
Repertoire: four-song mini-set with no tutorial.

## Month 4 exam

Technique: seventh-chord construction and 2-5-1 control.
Harmony: maj7/min7/dom7, walk-up/down, 2-5-1 in C/F/G.
Rhythm: one anticipatory or gospel-style movement without pulse loss.
Ear: triad vs 7th-colour distinctions.
Repertoire: one piece with intro/ending and one enriched arrangement.

## Month 5 exam

Technique: melody-plus-chords coordination.
Harmony: passing movement and transposition.
Rhythm: maintain groove across at least two keys.
Ear: by-ear project score at least 6/10 before source checking.
Repertoire: at least eight learned pieces total, five reasonably secure.

## Final exam, Week 26

### Technique
- all 12 major scales hands separately, one octave;
- priority keys C/G/D/A/F/Bb most fluent;
- major/minor triads in all keys from formula.

### Harmony
- inversions;
- 7ths, sus, add9;
- 1-4-5, 1-5-6-4, 6-4-1-5, 2-5-1 in several keys.

### Rhythm
- 90 seconds continuous playing with stable pulse;
- one accompaniment variation;
- mistake recovery without restart.

### Ear/transposition
- find tonic and opening note of two simple familiar choruses;
- identify likely bass movement;
- transpose one easy chorus C/F/G/D.

### Performance
Record 10-15 minutes including:
- one simple clean chorus;
- one inversion-based piece;
- one melody-plus-chords piece;
- one transposed piece;
- one piece learned substantially by ear.

## Advancement policy

A failed monthly test does not mean repeat the entire month. Identify the lowest domain, prescribe a two-week repair target, and continue only with new material that does not depend directly on the failed skill.
