# Repertoire Roadmap

The repertoire is intentionally introduced early. The purpose is motivational as well as technical: each chorus should make a new skill audible.

Do not chase polished arrangements from the beginning. First play the harmonic skeleton cleanly, then improve rhythm, inversions, bass movement and voicing over time.

## Month 1: Immediate reward

### Week 1: This Is the Day
Primary goal: find notes, keep pulse, play a simple melody and/or basic I-IV-V harmony in C.

### Week 2: What a Mighty God We Serve
Primary goal: simple chord changes, steady beat, basic left-hand root support.

### Week 3: He's Got the Whole World in His Hands
Primary goal: melody confidence and simple accompaniment.

### Week 4: Give Me Oil in My Lamp
Primary goal: chord switching without stopping; first month performance recording.

## Month 2: Chords and inversions

### Week 5: I Will Enter His Gates
Primary goal: I-IV-V-vi family in an easy key.

### Week 6: Amazing Grace
Primary goal: melody plus simple harmony; pedal restraint.

### Week 7: Blessed Assurance
Primary goal: inversions and smoother voice leading.

### Week 8: Standing on the Promises
Primary goal: rhythmic accompaniment and confident chord changes.

## Month 3: Number system and transposition

### Week 9: As the Deer
Primary goal: hear and play a common worship progression.

### Week 10: Give Thanks
Primary goal: number-system thinking rather than memorising letters only.

### Week 11: I Love You Lord
Primary goal: lyrical accompaniment, controlled pedal, dynamic phrasing.

### Week 12: Majesty
Primary goal: fuller left hand and inversion-based right hand.

### Week 13: Month-3 consolidation set
Play four previously learned choruses back-to-back with no tutorial open.

## Month 4: Gospel vocabulary

### Week 14: Oh How I Love Jesus
Primary goal: dominant colour, walk-up/walk-down awareness.

### Week 15: I Have Decided to Follow Jesus
Primary goal: rhythmic variety and chord anticipation.

### Week 16: To God Be the Glory
Primary goal: seventh chords and stronger cadences.

### Week 17: Great Is Thy Faithfulness
Primary goal: richer voicing without losing melodic clarity.

## Month 5: Ear, melody and harmony

### Week 18: As the Deer, reharmonised lightly
Primary goal: apply one or two passing movements only after the basic progression is secure.

### Week 19: Amazing Grace, melody plus chords
Primary goal: right-hand melody with supporting harmony.

### Week 20: Blessed Assurance, transpose one section
Primary goal: take a familiar chorus into a second key.

### Week 21: Learner-choice worship chorus
Primary goal: work out tonic, likely progression and melody partly by ear before using a tutorial.

### Week 22: Learner-choice gospel chorus
Primary goal: identify bass movement, number progression and cadence by ear.

## Month 6: Independence and performance

### Week 23: Transposition project
Take one easy chorus into C, F, G and D.

### Week 24: Arrangement project
Create a beginning, verse/chorus texture change and ending for one familiar chorus.

### Week 25: Five-song set
Choose five pieces from the course. Play them in sequence with no tutorial.

### Week 26: Final performance
Record 10-15 minutes of playing including:
1. one simple chorus played cleanly;
2. one song using inversions;
3. one piece with melody plus chords;
4. one transposed chorus;
5. one learner-choice piece learned substantially by ear.

## Repertoire rules

- A piece is not "learned" because one section was copied from YouTube.
- Keep a basic version permanently available. Fancy harmony is optional decoration.
- Once a chorus is learned, play it at least once per week for the next four weeks.
- Maintain a rotating set of 3-5 pieces that are always performance-ready.
- Do not add passing chords where the underlying progression is still uncertain.
