# Piano Ear-Training Ladder — 26 Weeks

Ear training is practical: hear musical function, predict sound, then verify at the keyboard.

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4
- higher/lower
- same/different
- step vs skip
- tonic/home feeling
- major vs minor quality

## Phase 2 — Weeks 5–8
- sing scale degrees 1–2–3–2–1 and 1–3–5
- hear 1→4, 1→5, 5→1
- identify bass movement among 1/4/5
- predict tonic before touching keyboard

## Phase 3 — Weeks 9–13
- recognize common 1–5–6–4 / 6–4–1–5 motion by bass direction
- major/minor diatonic chord quality
- identify 5→1 resolution
- find starting note of familiar melodies

## Phase 4 — Weeks 14–18
- triad vs maj7/min7/dom7 colour
- hear 2→5→1 tension/release
- sing destination bass notes before walk-ups/downs
- distinguish stable chord vs passing movement

## Phase 5 — Weeks 19–22
- identify melody top note within chord texture
- infer likely chord from melody/bass context
- find tonic and first bass movement of a familiar chorus before checking any source
- learn at least one chorus partly by ear

## Phase 6 — Weeks 23–26
- hear plain vs enriched voicings
- infer likely 1/4/5/6 functions in familiar styles
- identify cadence direction
- final test: find tonic and likely first progression of two simple songs before checking

## Weekly rule
Ear work comes before visual confirmation whenever possible: predict → play → compare → correct.

## Score 0–5
5 = predicts correctly and explains function
4 = correct after one check
3 = identifies broad direction/quality
2 = needs repeated trials
1 = guesses without usable strategy
0 = not attempted

Final goal: emerging functional hearing, not perfect pitch or advanced interval-label testing.
