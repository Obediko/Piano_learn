For every week:

- [ ] Completed Monday lesson

- [ ] Completed Saturday review
- [ ] Recorded at least one complete take

- [ ] Scored Saturday recording using `ASSESSMENTS.md`

## Month 1 test, end Week 4

- [ ] Find any named white key quickly across the keyboard
- [ ] Play C major five-finger pattern with both hands separately
- [ ] Play C major scale one octave hands separately
- [ ] Build C, F and G major triads without a diagram
- [ ] Play I-IV-V-I in C at 60 bpm without stopping
- [ ] Keep a basic 4/4 pulse
- [ ] Play at least two early choruses from memory
- [ ] Higher/lower ear score at least 8/10
- [ ] Major/minor ear score at least 7/10
- [ ] Monthly practical score at least 70/100, with no domain below 10/20

## Month 2 test, end Week 8

- [ ] Play C, G, F and D major scales hands separately
- [ ] Build major/minor triads in C, G, F and D
- [ ] Play root-position and first/second inversions of C, F and G
- [ ] Use inversions to reduce hand jumping
- [ ] Play 1-5-6-4 in at least two keys
- [ ] Use sustain pedal without blurring every chord
- [ ] Play four choruses from memory
- [ ] Monthly practical score at least 70/100

## Month 3 test, end Week 13

- [ ] Explain scale degrees 1-7
- [ ] Convert a simple chord progression between letter names and numbers
- [ ] Play 1-4-5, 1-5-6-4 and 6-4-1-5 in C, G, F and D
- [ ] Play C, G, D, A, F and Bb major scales
- [ ] Recognise tonic resolution by ear most of the time
- [ ] Transpose one easy chorus from C to G or F
- [ ] Play a four-chorus mini-set without opening YouTube
- [ ] Monthly practical score at least 70/100

## Month 4 test, end Week 17

- [ ] Build dominant 7, minor 7 and major 7 chords from a named root
- [ ] Use at least one walk-up or walk-down tastefully
- [ ] Play a basic 2-5-1 in C, F and G
- [ ] Use left-hand root/fifth or octave patterns without losing pulse
- [ ] Play one chorus with a clear intro and ending
- [ ] Distinguish major from minor chord quality by ear at better-than-chance level
- [ ] Monthly practical score at least 70/100

## Month 5 test, end Week 22

- [ ] Play melody plus chord support for at least one familiar song
- [ ] Transpose one chorus into at least three keys
- [ ] Work out tonic of a simple song by ear
- [ ] Guess bass movement of a simple chorus before checking a tutorial
- [ ] Use one passing chord/secondary movement without losing destination harmony
- [ ] Play at least eight pieces in total, with five reasonably secure
- [ ] By-ear project score at least 6/10 before source checking
- [ ] Monthly practical score at least 70/100

## Final test, Week 26

- [ ] Play all 12 major scales hands separately; priority keys more fluently
- [ ] Build major/minor triads in all keys from formulas
- [ ] Use inversions fluently in common progressions
- [ ] Play dominant 7, minor 7, major 7, sus and add9 in practical contexts
- [ ] Play 1-4-5, 1-5-6-4, 6-4-1-5 and 2-5-1 in several keys
- [ ] Keep time with metronome/backing pulse for 90 seconds
- [ ] Play at least five pieces without tutorial dependence
- [ ] Transpose one chorus into C/F/G/D
- [ ] Learn one simple chorus substantially by ear
- [ ] Recover from one mistake without restarting
- [ ] Record a 10-15 minute final performance
- [ ] Final practical score at least 70/100
