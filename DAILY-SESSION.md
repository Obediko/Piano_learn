# Daily Session Operating System

Use this file every practice day before opening YouTube. The purpose is to prevent passive watching and make each 60-minute block measurable.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Start with the keyboard, not YouTube.
2. Never spend more than 15-20 minutes of a 60-minute session watching instruction.
3. Every concept must be reproduced from memory before the session ends.
4. If tension or pain appears, stop, reset posture and reduce speed.
5. Do not increase tempo after a lucky clean repetition. Earn it with three clean repetitions.
6. Never sacrifice pulse for speed.
7. End every session with a short performance, not another explanation video.

## Standard 60-minute session

### 0-5 min: reset and warm-up
- Sit so forearms are roughly level with the keys.
- Shoulders relaxed, wrists neutral, fingers naturally curved.
- Play slow five-finger patterns or the week's easiest scale.
- Check that no finger is collapsing and no wrist is locked.

### 5-15 min: technique
Follow `TECHNIQUE-MAP.md`.
- scale/five-finger pattern;
- chord-shape or inversion drill;
- one hand-independence task.

### 15-25 min: harmony/theory at the keyboard
- build the week's chords from note names or formulas;
- say scale degrees aloud;
- play the week's progression slowly;
- do not look at a chord diagram after the first successful construction.

### 25-40 min: guided lesson
Use the video named in `CURRICULUM.md` or `VIDEO-LIBRARY.md`.

Protocol:
1. Watch one short explanation.
2. Pause.
3. Reproduce the smallest musical unit five correct times.
4. Combine two units.
5. Close the video.
6. Play the idea from memory.

### 40-55 min: repertoire
Use `SONGBOOK.md` and `REPERTOIRE.md`.
- first play the easiest version;
- then add the week's new skill;
- preserve pulse even if the arrangement must be simplified.

### 55-60 min: ear test + log
- do the week's ear task from `EAR-TRAINING.md`;
- record 30-60 seconds if useful;
- write: `Today I improved...`; `My main error was...`; `Tomorrow I will fix...`.

## 30-minute emergency version

Use when tired or busy. Do not skip completely unless genuinely necessary.

- 5 min technique
- 5 min chord/progression drill
- 10 min hardest current problem
- 10 min chorus from memory

No browsing for new tutorials on a 30-minute day.

## Tempo ladder

For any drill with a metronome:

1. Find a tempo where you can play correctly with relaxed hands.
2. Complete 3 clean repetitions.
3. Increase by 5 bpm.
4. If you make 2 errors within 3 attempts, drop 5-10 bpm.
5. Log the highest clean tempo, not the fastest attempted tempo.

A clean repetition means:
- correct notes/chords;
- correct fingering where specified;
- uninterrupted pulse;
- no visible strain;
- no restart.

## Daily pass rule

A day is complete only if you can answer yes to at least four of these five:

- [ ] I can demonstrate today's main skill without the video.
- [ ] I can play today's drill three times without stopping.
- [ ] I kept a steady pulse for at least 30 seconds.
- [ ] I applied the skill to the current chorus/song.
- [ ] I can state what tomorrow's correction should be.

If fewer than four are true, mark the day `REPEAT` and begin the next session with 10 minutes on the failed skill.

## Saturday protocol

Saturday contains no compulsory new lesson.

1. 10 min technique review.
2. 10 min progression review.
3. 10 min ear/rhythm test.
4. 20 min repertoire set.
5. 10 min recording + scoring.

Score the recording with `ASSESSMENTS.md`.
