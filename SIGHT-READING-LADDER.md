# Piano Sight-Reading Ladder — 26 Weeks

Sight-reading is trained as a small daily skill, not a separate theory subject.

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4
- keyboard geography
- middle C and landmark notes
- finger numbers
- quarter, half and whole notes
- seconds and thirds by interval shape
- 5 minutes/day, one hand at a time

## Phase 2 — Weeks 5–8
- treble/bass landmarks
- steps vs skips
- five-finger patterns
- simple two-measure phrases
- rests and bar lines
- hands separately at steady pulse

## Phase 3 — Weeks 9–13
- interval reading up to fifths
- chord stacks as shapes
- simple LH roots + RH melody
- 4–8 bar beginner pieces
- count aloud before playing

## Phase 4 — Weeks 14–18
- lead-sheet symbols + notation together
- seventh-chord symbols
- repeated rhythmic patterns
- melody with chord support
- simple hymn/chorus excerpts

## Phase 5 — Weeks 19–22
- melody-top-note awareness
- bass movement reading
- simple two-hand coordination
- sight-read in multiple familiar keys
- avoid restarting after mistakes

## Phase 6 — Weeks 23–26
- 8–16 bar unfamiliar material
- 30–60 seconds preview, then one uninterrupted attempt
- simple lead sheet + notated melody
- final target: preserve pulse and recover after errors

## Daily rule
5–10 minutes maximum. Reading should be easier than repertoire.

## Score 0–5
5 = steady pulse, mostly correct notes, no restart
4 = small errors, pulse preserved
3 = several hesitations but form survives
2 = frequent stops
1 = cannot proceed without note-by-note decoding
0 = not attempted

Final target: average >=4 on beginner material in familiar keys.
