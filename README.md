# Six-Month Piano Learning Curriculum

**Dates:** 14 September 2026 to 13 March 2027  
**Schedule:** Monday-Saturday, 20:00-21:00 (Europe/Berlin)  
**Sunday:** Rest  
**Primary direction:** practical piano, gospel/worship, chord-based playing, ear development and solid foundational musicianship.

## Goal

Move from beginner level to a solid practical lower-intermediate foundation in 26 weeks. The aim is not to copy tutorials indefinitely. By the end, you should be able to navigate the keyboard confidently, build chords, use common progressions, play several choruses from memory, transpose simple material, recognise common harmonic movement by ear, use inversions and basic gospel vocabulary, and learn a simple song with much less dependence on a tutorial.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Monday:** new foundation or technique.
- **Tuesday:** reinforce it under a metronome.
- **Wednesday:** musical application or second concept.
- **Thursday:** ear training, rhythm, reading, transposition or theory at the keyboard.
- **Friday:** repertoire and chorus integration.
- **Saturday:** practice/review only. No new material. Record yourself.
- **Sunday:** rest.

## Standard 60-minute practice block

1. 5 min posture, relaxation and warm-up
2. 10 min scale/finger technique
3. 10 min chords/theory at the keyboard
4. 15 min guided lesson/video
5. 15 min repertoire/chorus
6. 5 min ear training and practice log

### Compressed 30-minute day

5 min technique + 5 min chords/theory + 10 min focused drill + 10 min repertoire. Never use the whole practice session merely watching videos.

## Advancement rule

Watching a video does not count as mastery. A skill counts only when you can reproduce it at the keyboard without pausing the tutorial.

By Saturday, aim to demonstrate:

- the week's key/scale at the required level;
- the week's progression without stopping;
- one chorus or song section from memory;
- one ear-training or transposition task;
- one 60-120 second recording.

If a checkpoint is not met, continue the course but carry the weak skill into the following week's warm-up until it is reliable.
