# Piano Learn: Six-Month Beginner-to-Practical-Player System

**Dates:** 14 September 2026 to 13 March 2027  
**Practice window:** Monday-Saturday, 20:00-21:00 (Europe/Berlin)  
**Sunday:** Rest  
**Primary direction:** practical piano, gospel/worship, chord-based playing, ear development, rhythm, transposition and solid foundational musicianship.

## What this repository is

This is not a YouTube playlist. It is a 26-week training system built around deliberate practice.

The course combines:
- keyboard technique;
- scale and chord literacy;
- rhythm and groove;
- ear training;
- number-system harmony;
- inversions and voice leading;
- gospel/contemporary vocabulary;
- melody-plus-chords playing;
- transposition;
- repertoire retention;
- practical performance tests.

Choruses begin in Week 1 so the course remains musically rewarding while technique develops.

## Goal after six months

A successful learner should be able to:
- navigate the keyboard confidently;
- play all major scales at least hands separately, with priority keys more fluent;
- build major/minor triads from formulas;
- use inversions in common progressions;
- play common 1/4/5/6 progressions in several keys;
- use dominant 7, minor 7, major 7, sus and add9 in practical contexts;
- keep a stable pulse and recover from mistakes without restarting;
- accompany several choruses from memory;
- transpose a simple chorus through C/F/G/D;
- find tonic and basic bass movement by ear;
- learn the skeleton of a simple familiar chorus before opening a tutorial;
- perform a 10-15 minute final set without tutorial dependence.

This is a strong practical lower-intermediate foundation, not professional mastery.

## Advancement rules

Watching a lesson is not mastery.

A skill counts only when you can demonstrate it without real-time tutorial dependence.

Daily pass: at least 4/5 criteria in `DAILY-SESSION.md`.

Saturday interpretation:
- 90-100: excellent
- 80-89: secure
- 70-79: functional but fragile, carry one repair target
- 60-69: repair before adding complexity to that skill
- below 60: simplify and rebuild

Monthly exam pass: at least 70/100 overall, with no domain below 10/20.

## The anti-tutorial-dependence rule

For every video:
1. watch one small explanation;
2. pause;
3. reproduce it five correct times;
4. close the video;
5. play from memory;
6. apply it to the week's music.

The course is working when YouTube gradually becomes a reference rather than a crutch.

## Missed practice

Do not cram missed days.
- one missed day: continue normally;
- two missed days: 30 min review + 30 min current material;
- three or more: do a Saturday-style review, find the last secure skill, then resume.

See `RECOVERY.md`.

## Final standard

Week 26 ends with a 10-15 minute recorded performance including:
- a clean simple chorus;
- an inversion-based piece;
- melody plus chords;
- a transposed chorus;
- a piece learned substantially by ear.

The final question is not "How many tutorials did I finish?" It is "What can I now play, hear, explain and reproduce without the tutorial open?"
